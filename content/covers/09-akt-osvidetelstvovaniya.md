## Название концепции

Круг, в котором не хватает одного

## Готовый промт

```
Wide cinematic film still at dusk on an unfinished concrete floor slab.
Four empty folding chairs stand in a loose circle around a low trestle
table in the centre of the frame; three of them are pushed in and dusted
with concrete powder, the fourth is knocked back and turned away, its seat
clean, as if its occupant never arrived. On the table, a rolled bundle of
drawings weighed down by a steel bolt. A lone worker in a hard hat, seen
from behind in silhouette, stands at the far edge of the circle with his
hands at his sides, waiting. Beyond him, tower cranes and open floor edges
recede into volumetric haze under a burning amber sky. Strong backlight
from the open facade, long chair shadows raking across the slab, deep
blue-grey shadow in the foreground. Textures of raw concrete, powdered
dust, scuffed metal tubing, worn canvas, rolled paper. Atmosphere of a
decision that cannot be made because one authority is missing. No readable
text, no logos, no watermarks, no visible face, photorealistic, cinematic
film still, high detail.
```

## Смысловая метафора

Акт освидетельствования держится не на бланке, а на составе подписантов:
подпись человека без полномочий равна её отсутствию. Кадр показывает не
документ, а собрание, которое не состоялось, — и пустой стул, из-за
которого всё остальное не имеет силы.

---

## Запасная концепция

Если основной кадр выйдет слишком постановочным — генератор любит
превращать сцену в переговорную с кофе.

**Название концепции:** Цепь, у которой вынуто звено

```
Extreme close cinematic film still at dusk on a construction site. A heavy
rusted steel chain runs diagonally across the frame under visible tension,
each link scarred and wet; at the centre of the frame one link is missing
entirely and the two free ends hang apart by a hand's width, still pulling.
Behind the chain, deeply out of focus, a lone worker in a hard hat seen
from behind in silhouette walks away between concrete columns. Burning
amber backlight through construction haze, deep blue-grey shadows, dust in
the beams. Textures of pitted rust, oil, wet steel, raw concrete.
Atmosphere of a load that is about to be released with nothing holding it.
No readable text, no logos, no watermarks, no visible face, photorealistic,
cinematic film still, high detail.
```

Читается как «цепочка согласования рвётся в одном месте»: сильнее как
метафора, но дальше от темы собрания. Брать, если первый вариант не
сработает.
